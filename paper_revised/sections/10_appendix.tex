The replication package holds the components of every prompt as separate files in the folder \texttt{revision/prompts/}: the three system messages of Listing~\ref{lst:sys}, the one-shot template of Listing~\ref{lst:prompt}, the zero-shot template of C1, the story and reference Gherkin of each of the six exemplars (subfolder \texttt{exemplars/}), and the mapping of Table~\ref{tab:prompts} (\texttt{exemplar\_mapping.csv}). A C0 or C2 prompt is the one-shot template with the story and Gherkin of the project's exemplar in place of \texttt{\{example\_user\_story\}} and \texttt{\{example\_gherkin\}}. The script \texttt{revision/prompts/make\_prompt\_artifacts.py} rebuilds these files from the dataset and the generation code without calling the API. It checks that the system messages equal those of the original notebook and that the C0 prompts it assembles equal those of the notebook for all 261 stories. All files use LF line breaks, and their SHA-256 hashes are listed below (in \texttt{MANIFEST\_SHA256.txt} the exemplar files carry the prefix \texttt{exemplars/}). C0 was run on 19 July 2026 and C1 and C2 on 16 October 2026, as recorded in the API logs. In those logs the prompt-token difference between C0 or C2 and C1 is the same for every file of a project (C0: 271, 1,910 and 299 tokens for Sylius, Fineract and Diaspora; C2: 960, 9,413 and 965), which is consistent with one fixed exemplar block per project. The version used for this paper is the release tag \texttt{fisat2026-camera-ready}, \url{https://github.com/simonhoang611/RT-SWT-004-Group4/tree/fisat2026-camera-ready}.

\section{Perturbation Test}
\label{app:perturb}

\textbf{Design.} The perturbation test measured whether the metric reacts to seeded defects. From the reference files, 120 scenarios (40 per project, fixed seed) were extracted, and eight operators were applied where applicable: changing a number, a quoted value, a comparison operator or a negation, dropping a Then or a Given step, swapping a When with a Then, and moving a Then to the front. Each mutant was compared with its original scenario using the same skeleton metric.

\textbf{Results.} The 120 extracted scenarios yielded 683 applicable mutants. Of these, 29 mutants do not parse, and the extraction omitted the Background of 74 of the 120 source scenarios. Restricting to the 623 pairs in which both texts parse and the original has no keyword-order flag, every mutant keeps a skeleton cosine of at least 0.8995 with its original. At 0.90, 622 of 623 mutants would still pass, and at 0.95, 97.9\%. For changed numbers and quoted values (179 pairs) the skeletons are identical, which follows from masking and is not an empirical finding. Swapped When/Then steps and Then steps moved to the front (210 pairs) score at least 0.954, although the keyword-order check flags all of them. Because the test compares single scenarios and the main metric compares whole files, these results show insensitivity at the scenario level and do not estimate a false-negative rate for whole files.
