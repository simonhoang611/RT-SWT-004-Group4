\section{Threats to Validity}
\label{sec:threats}

For each threat we report what we did and the residual risk.

\subsection{Internal Validity}

\textbf{Reverse-engineered stories.} \emph{Mitigation:} we measured trigram overlap and prefix-story performance (Sections~\ref{sec:res-b1} and~\ref{sec:res-h1}). \emph{Residual risk:} overlap correlates with similarity in two projects, structural leakage is unmeasured, and performance may be overstated for natural requirements; no independently written backlog was available.

\textbf{Exemplars, pilot and hosted model.} \emph{Mitigation:} prompt exemplars are excluded from the primary analysis; C0--C2 were rerun with interleaved requests, calls were logged, and run variation and the planned 1,500-token C0cap condition were measured. \emph{Residual risk:} the 30 pilot stories are in the evaluated set, C2 uses one random exemplar draw, nondeterminism remains, and hosted serving changes cannot be detected. Under C0cap, long outputs were cut and parser compliance fell to 92.6\%; the uncapped RQ2 result does not apply to that condition.

\subsection{Construct Validity}

\textbf{Similarity.} \emph{Mitigation:} we compared cosine with defect labels, seeded perturbations, project results and reference points from 0.80 to 0.90. \emph{Residual risk:} masking hides values, tables are omitted, many skeletons exceed the 256-token limit, and cosine did not separate defective from non-defective audited files.

\textbf{Defect labels.} \emph{Mitigation:} one independent rater labelled 15 files; we report both $\kappa$ values with CIs and held the required consensus round. \emph{Residual risk:} labels were AI-drafted and reviewed in one confirmation; agreement is below 0.7 and imprecise; the other 45 files were not relabelled. The audit is exploratory and limited to files scoring at least 0.85.

\textbf{Parser and perturbation.} \emph{Mitigation:} parser compliance uses \texttt{behave}; non-parsing mutant pairs were excluded. \emph{Residual risk:} mutants are dependent single scenarios and do not estimate whole-file sensitivity; parser success does not execute steps or establish behavior.

\subsection{External and Conclusion Validity}

\textbf{Projects and reference point.} \emph{Mitigation:} we report per-project results and the 0.80--0.90 sensitivity analysis. \emph{Residual risk:} three public English-language projects are not representative of all BDD practice; 0.85 is uncalibrated, and the RQ1 decision changes between 0.84 and 0.85. Planned-after-review analyses are exploratory and their $p$-values are not corrected.

The study had no independently written requirements, could not detect hosted-service changes, did not execute generated files against step definitions, and could not relabel the 45 files outside consensus. Conclusions are limited to reverse-engineered stories from these projects during the observed run window, with skeleton cosine treated only as a proxy.
