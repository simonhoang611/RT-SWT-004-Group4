# Inclusion and Exclusion (IC/EC) Criteria

## Inclusion Criteria (IC)
*A paper is INCLUDED only if it answers YES to ALL of these:*

- **IC1**: Paper is written in English.
- **IC2**: Published from 2018 onwards.
- **IC3**: Peer-reviewed (journal or conference paper).
- **IC4**: Relevant to automated test generation, Gherkin/BDD, or LLMs in Software Engineering.
- **IC5**: Contains experimental results/empirical data (not just a survey, opinion, or theoretical proposal).

## Exclusion Criteria (EC)
*A paper is EXCLUDED if it answers YES to ANY of these:*

- **EC1**: Duplicate paper (already included).
- **EC2**: Full-text PDF cannot be found or accessed.
- **EC3**: It is a poster, extended abstract, or a short paper (< 4 pages).
- **EC4**: Focuses only on test execution/maintenance, without covering test generation.
